    - **A:** No, this is not acceptable for active development.     - **A:** No, you should reject it. The instructor warned that port 22 is a special port reserved for secure connections on most machines.     - **A:** No, this is not good practice. The instructor explained that containers are essentially other computers running in the background, consuming RAM.
